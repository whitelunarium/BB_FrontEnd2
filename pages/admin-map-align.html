Map alignment tool

Two modes: Quick (click each painted number in order — fastest) or Drag (reposition individual markers). Export when done.

Ready
Quick mode: The side panel shows one neighborhood at a time. Look at the official map for the painted number (e.g. "8"), then click that spot on the map. The tool advances to the next one automatically. When you've done all 60, click Download JSON, then paste it into the live editor (assets/data/poway-neighborhoods.json) or send it to your developer.
