Support PNEC's Mission

PNEC is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that operates entirely on donations and volunteer effort. Every dollar goes directly toward training, equipment, and keeping Poway's neighborhoods prepared.

📚

Training Materials

CERT manuals, first-aid cards, and preparedness guides for every coordinator and workshop participant.

🎒

CERT Equipment

Hard hats, safety vests, first-aid kits, and search lights for CERT response teams.

🏘️

Community Outreach

Neighborhood fliers, multilingual materials, and door-to-door coordinator campaigns.

📻

Radio Infrastructure

PACT amateur radios, repeater maintenance, and equipment for emergency communications.
