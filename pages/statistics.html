Wildfire Risk in Poway

Poway sits in one of California's highest fire-risk zones. CAL FIRE designates most of the city as Very High Fire Hazard Severity, reflecting the region's dry brush, steep terrain, and Santa Ana winds.

Flood & Heat Risk

Poway's canyon geography creates localized flash flood risk during heavy rain events. Extreme heat episodes are also increasing in frequency across inland San Diego County.

Zone X FEMA flood map designation for most of Poway — moderate to low risk, but flash flooding can occur outside mapped zones
110°F+ Peak heat recorded in Poway area during recent inland heat events
15–40 min Flash flood warning lead time — far shorter than wildfire evacuation windows
