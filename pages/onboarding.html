👋

Welcome to PNEC!

You've just joined the Poway Neighborhood Emergency Corps community. This takes about 60 seconds — let's show you what's here.

PNEC is an all-volunteer nonprofit that helps Poway residents prepare for wildfires, earthquakes, and floods. Your account unlocks personalized tools.

🛰️

Live Risk Watch

The homepage shows a live risk panel that updates every 30 minutes with real weather data. You'll see scores for:

  • Wildfire risk — based on temperature, humidity, wind
  • Flood risk — based on recent rainfall and saturation
  • Heat risk — based on current heat index

When risk is elevated, prepare early. Don't wait for an official alert.

🏘️

Your Neighborhood

Each Poway neighborhood has a trained Emergency Coordinator — a volunteer who connects you to local preparedness activities and resources.

If you didn't set your neighborhood during registration, you can update it anytime on your profile page.

60+ Poway neighborhoods are in the PNEC network. Finding yours connects you to the right local resources.

💬

PNEC Helper Chatbot

See the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of every page? That's the PNEC Helper — an AI assistant trained on PNEC's preparedness resources.

  • Ask about emergency kits, evacuation routes, or wildfire history
  • Get instant answers from PNEC's FAQ database
  • If it can't help, send a direct question to PNEC staff
🎒

Build Your Preparedness Plan

Use the site to take practical steps before an emergency happens:

  • Make a go-bag with water, food, medication, documents, masks, and chargers
  • Review evacuation readiness with your household before wildfire season
  • Find your neighborhood so PNEC can connect you with the right local resources
  • Ask PNEC Helper when you need a quick answer or a staff follow-up

Preparedness works best when residents complete small steps before conditions become urgent.

✅

You're all set!

Here's what to do next:

  • Check the live risk panel on the homepage
  • Visit Programs & Services to explore CERT training and volunteering
  • Complete your profile and keep your neighborhood status current
  • Use Preparedness Resources to build your household plan
