RISK WATCH

Live warnings for possible natural disaster risk in Poway

This section uses live environmental data from the risk API to estimate short-term wildfire, flood, and extreme heat danger. Scores are based on current conditions such as temperature, humidity, wind, rainfall, and heat index to help residents see when local risk may be rising.

What this means
Higher scores indicate a stronger likelihood that weather conditions could contribute to dangerous events. Use this as an early warning signal and review your preparedness plan when risk climbs.
